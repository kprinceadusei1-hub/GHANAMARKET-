To keep the marketplace legal and safe, some items cannot be listed, and others need extra checks.

Not allowed

  • Illegal drugs, controlled substances and drug paraphernalia.
  • Firearms, ammunition, explosives and weapons.
  • Stolen goods, or items you do not have the right to sell.
  • Counterfeit or replica branded goods.
  • Prescription-only medicines.
  • Human remains or body parts, and protected wildlife or wildlife products.
  • Hacked accounts, personal data, and fake documents or certificates.
  • Adult sexual content or services.
  • Anything that promotes fraud, gambling schemes or pyramid schemes.

Restricted

Some categories, such as vehicles and property, need extra care. Listings in restricted categories receive closer review, and our team may ask for supporting documents.

Listings that break these rules are removed, and repeated violations can lead to account suspension. If you think a decision was wrong, use the appeal form.

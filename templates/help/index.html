Quick answers

Is it free to post an ad?

Yes. Posting listings is free during the launch period. See fees.

Why is my ad not visible yet?

Every new listing, and every edit, is reviewed by our team before buyers can see it. You can follow the status in My shop.

What does “Buy online” mean?

The seller accepts payment through GhanaMarket with Mobile Money or card. Your order is only marked as paid after the payment provider confirms the payment to our server.

What does “Verified seller” mean?

A GhanaMarket team member has checked the seller's Ghana Card or business registration. It confirms identity, not the quality of every item, so still inspect what you buy.

My account was suspended. What can I do?

Use the appeal form. You do not need to be signed in.

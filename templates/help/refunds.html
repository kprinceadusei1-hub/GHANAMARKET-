How cancellations, returns and refunds work for orders paid online through GhanaMarket.

Before you pay

You can cancel a checkout at any time before paying. Nothing is charged, and reserved items are released.

After you pay

  • If the seller cancels, you are refunded in full through the payment provider you paid with.
  • If you want to cancel, return an item, get a refund, or dispute an order, open a request from the order page. The seller and our support team can see it and will respond.
  • Refunds go back to the Mobile Money wallet or card you paid with. How long it takes to arrive depends on the payment provider and your bank or network.

Deals arranged by chat

When you pay a seller directly, outside online checkout, GhanaMarket does not hold or process the money and cannot reverse the payment. Follow our safety tips, and report any problem so we can act against the account.

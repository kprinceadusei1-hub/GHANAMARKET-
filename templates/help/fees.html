Clear pricing with no surprises.

For sellers

  • Posting listings is free during the launch period.
  • There is currently no commission on sales.
  • If this changes, fees will be announced in advance and shown here before they apply.
  • Promoted listings: sellers can ask our team to promote a listing so it appears first on the homepage or in its category. Any price is agreed in writing before the promotion starts; there is no payment for promotions through the website yet. Promoted listings are always labelled “Promoted”.

For buyers

  • You pay the listed price plus any delivery fee. Every fee is shown at checkout before you pay.
  • Collection from the seller is free.

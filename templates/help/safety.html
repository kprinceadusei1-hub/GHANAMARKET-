Most deals go smoothly. These habits keep it that way.

When you buy from a listing you chat about

  • Never send Mobile Money or pay a “deposit” before you see the item. Scammers often ask for a small advance to “hold” an item.
  • Meet in a busy public place in daylight, such as a mall, bank or police station car park. Bring a friend for high-value items.
  • Inspect the item carefully and test electronics. For phones, check the IMEI and that the phone is not locked to an account.
  • For vehicles and property, confirm documents in person and never pay to see a property.
  • Keep your conversation on GhanaMarket. If someone pushes you to move to another app straight away, be careful.

When you use online checkout

  • Only pay on the GhanaMarket checkout page or the payment provider's page it sends you to. Sellers should never ask you to pay them separately for the same order.
  • Your order shows as paid only after the payment provider confirms it to us.
  • If there is a problem, open a request from the order page so there is a record.

Warning signs

  • A price far below market value.
  • Pressure to decide or pay immediately.
  • Requests for your PIN, password or verification codes. GhanaMarket staff will never ask for these.

If something goes wrong

Report the listing or the user from the listing or conversation page, and contact support. If you have lost money, also report it to the police and your Mobile Money provider quickly.

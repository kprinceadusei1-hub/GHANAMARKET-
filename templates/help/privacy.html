What we collect, who can see it, and how to remove it.

What we collect

  • Account details: username, name, email, phone number, city and region.
  • Listings: photos, descriptions and location you publish.
  • Messages: conversations about listings, so we can act on reports.
  • Orders: items, delivery details, and payment status and references. Card details are entered on the payment provider's page and never reach GhanaMarket.
  • Seller verification: the document and number you submit, stored privately and visible only to authorised staff.

Who can see what

  • Buyers see your public name, city and listings. Your phone number and email are not shown publicly.
  • When you buy online, the seller sees the delivery name, phone number and address for that order so they can deliver.
  • Authorised staff can see account, listing, message, order and verification data to review listings, handle reports and support you.

Your choices

  • Update your details in account settings.
  • Delete your account from account settings. Your personal details are removed. Order and payment records are kept without your contact details for accounting and dispute resolution.
  • For any other privacy request, contact support.

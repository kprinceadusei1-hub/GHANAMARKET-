The basic rules for using GhanaMarket.

  1. Give accurate information about yourself and the items you list.
  2. Only list items you own or are allowed to sell, and never list prohibited items.
  3. Use honest photos and descriptions, including any faults.
  4. Treat other users with respect. Harassment, threats and scams lead to removal.
  5. For orders paid online, sellers must fulfil the order as described or cancel it so the buyer is refunded.
  6. For deals arranged by chat, the buyer and seller agree payment and handover directly. GhanaMarket is not a party to those deals and does not hold the money.
  7. We review listings and may remove content or suspend accounts that break these rules. You can appeal any decision.
